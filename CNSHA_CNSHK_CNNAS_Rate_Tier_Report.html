Management Analysis

More analysis and improvement suggestions for management review

Action Focus
CNSHA
Structure is the healthiest among the 3 ports. T1 = 74.0% of TEU and Spot = 5.1%, so the priority is not rescue but scale discipline.
CNSHK
Mix quality is under pressure. Spot = 32.7% of TEU, which is even higher than FAK and far above T1/T2, so the issue is quality of cargo mix, not volume alone.
CNNAS
Conversion opportunity is the main theme. T2 = 39.7% and T1 = 0%, meaning there is buying power but almost no premium lock-in yet.

What needs attention now

  • 1. CNSHK Spot exposure is the biggest drag. With 80 TEU in Spot, this is the clearest short-term margin leakage point.
  • 2. CNNAS has conversion upside. T2 base is already large enough to support an upgrade program into T1.
  • 3. CNSHA should be protected. Avoid letting healthy T1 cargo slide back into tactical discounting just to chase extra volume.

Suggested improvement actions

  • CNSHA: identify the most stable T1 / near-T1 accounts and push annual or semi-committed FAK conversion.
  • CNSHK: split Spot cargo by POD and customer, then target the repeat Spot accounts for Spot → T2 migration first.
  • CNNAS: launch a trial program on the strongest T2 customers to test T2 → T1 upgrade thresholds.
  • Commercial control: review all Spot-heavy pods weekly and flag any lane where Spot exceeds 25% of total TEU.
